🎯 Why This Prediction? (Purpose & Benefit)

We are addressing a critical gap: standard navigation apps (like Google Maps) optimize solely for **distance or travel time**, ignoring safety. Our GNN model offers tangible benefits:

  • **Public Safety:** Identifies and prioritizes dangerous street segments for infrastructure upgrades.
  • **User Experience:** Provides users with a **Safety Score** for routes, enabling informed choices (e.g., choosing a slightly longer but significantly safer path).
  • **Forecasting:** The GNN's analysis of features allows us to **forecast accidents before they happen**.

🧠 Model Approach (How It Works)

Our method is rooted in **Social Network Analysis**. We treat the road network as a **Graph** ($G$) and use the GNN to capture the **relational dependencies** between streets.

  • **GNN Core:** Uses **Message Passing** to aggregate features and risk scores from neighboring segments.
  • **The Output:** The model finds the **Shortest Accident Risk Path**, minimizing the cumulative predicted risk score (our GNN weight) instead of minimizing distance.
